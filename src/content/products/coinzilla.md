# Coinzilla Review

## Executive Summary

Coinzilla is an online digital advertising network that specializes in the financial, cryptocurrency, and blockchain sectors. Established to connect digital advertisers with relevant web traffic publishers, the platform offers native advertising, banner ads, and pop-under ads. It is frequently utilized by crypto projects, exchanges, and financial service providers seeking niche audience reach within the web3 ecosystem. 

## Key Facts

- **Industry Focus:** Cryptocurrencies, finance, blockchain, and fintech.
- **Ad Formats:** Banner ads, native advertising, and pop-under ads.
- **Target Audience:** Advertisers looking for crypto/finance traffic and publishers monetizing related websites.
- **Payment Methods:** Supports various payment options, historically including traditional methods and cryptocurrencies. Exact current payment rails are subject to change.
- **Verification Requirement:** Accounts and ad campaigns generally undergo a review process before going live.

## Pros & Cons

### Pros

- Tailored specifically for the cryptocurrency and financial niche.
- Multiple ad formats available to suit different campaign strategies.
- Self-serve platform interface for managing campaigns and budgets.
- Access to a dedicated network of finance- and crypto-oriented publishers.

### Cons

- Strict compliance policies can lead to campaign rejections if ads do not meet platform standards.
- Niche focus means traffic is limited outside of finance and crypto verticals.
- Competition for ad space within the crypto sector can affect pricing and reach.

## Key Features

- **Self-Serve Advertiser Dashboard:** Allows users to create, manage, and monitor the performance of their ad campaigns, tracking metrics such as impressions, clicks, and costs.
- **Publisher Monetization Tools:** Provides publishers with integration codes to display banner and native ads on their websites to generate revenue.
- **Multiple Ad Formats:** Supports standard IAB banner sizes, native ad widgets designed to blend with website content, and pop-under advertisements.
- **Targeting Options:** Basic geo-targeting and device targeting capabilities to help focus ad delivery on specific demographics or regions.
