# vultr Review

## Key Facts

- Vultr was founded in 2014 by David Aninowsky and is headquartered in West Palm Beach, Florida.

- Vultr operates data centers across 32 worldwide regions, covering North America, South America, Europe, Asia, the Middle East, Sub-Saharan Africa, and Australia.

- Vultr supports over 1.5 million customers across 185 countries with over 45 million instances deployed.

- Vultr is building a 50-megawatt cluster of AMD artificial intelligence processors at an Ohio data center, planning to spend over $1 billion on the facility.
