# serverspace Review

## Key Facts

- Serverspace is an international cloud provider offering automated virtual infrastructure deployment.

- Linux- or Windows-based virtual servers can be deployed in less than a minute.

- Serverspace operates within the ITGLOBAL.COM Group of Companies and is headquartered in Amsterdam, Netherlands.

- Serverspace data centers correspond to Tier III reliability standards.

- Serverspace has data centers across countries including the Netherlands, the USA, Canada, Kazakhstan, Turkey, the UAE, and Brazil.

## Pricing

## Pros

- Fast automated deployment of Linux or Windows servers in less than 1 minute.

- 10 Gbps DDoS filtering included without additional charge.

- Flexible billing allowing users to pause servers and pay only for disk/IP, with 10-minute or hourly increments.

- Offers a trial period.

- Tier III data center reliability minimizing downtime.

## Cons

- Basic backups are not included in the standard VPS cost and require a separate fee.

- Additional IPv4 addresses require an extra fee, and free blocks may temporarily run out.
